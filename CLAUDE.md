# Atlas — working notes for Claude

Atlas is Benji's personal life dashboard: one file, `index.html` (vanilla JS + CSS, no build step), deployed by GitHub Pages from `main`. Live at https://caesar-benji.github.io/atlas/. Backend is Supabase (project `gsyrqhoutpshtbzastyq`): email+password auth, every table has owner-only RLS with `user_id` defaulting to `auth.uid()`. Sections: Schedule (incl. the creatine/protein intake program), Gym, Money, Rent, Goals, Chores, House (floor planner + 3D view). The hub is a shallow 3D stage (v61): `.hub-stage` has perspective, `.hub-orbit` is preserve-3d and rotates toward the pointer via `--px/--py` (set by a pointermove handler) or sways on touch (`hubSway`); disc at translateZ(-40px), figure +50px, greeting +30px, tiles +90px. Never put a `filter: drop-shadow` on the masked figure image: inside the 3D layer Chromium clips it to the box. The hub greets with one line ("Good evening Benji") and the date under it; the intro (`atlasIntro`) plays on every cold load over the login or the hub, and again when the app returns to the foreground after 20+ minutes away or from the back-forward cache (`introOnReturn`; never over a running workout or an open sheet). Skippable by a tap; not gated by reduced-motion since v57. Phones mostly resume the page rather than reload it, which is why the return trigger exists. Design history and decisions live in the claude.ai Project "Personal App - Atlas" (docs `claude/atlas-v*.md`); keep a short note there or here when you change something structural.

## Conventions that matter

- **Bump the version on every deploy**: `<span id="ver">vN</span>` in the hub footer and `console.log('Atlas vN')` near the top of the script. GitHub Pages sometimes fails silently and the CDN caches ~10 min — check `#ver` on the live site after pushing.
- **No emojis in the UI.** One SVG icon set (`ICO`), no text ✕ / ✓.
- **No browser dialogs.** `confirm()`/`prompt()` are banned; use `ask({title, body, ok, cancel, danger})` (Promise-returning sheet) and the `.sheet` pattern. None remain as of v55 (rename and password reset use sheets).
- **Palette** is a paper-white e-book look: tokens on `:root` (`--bg`, `--surface`, `--accent`, muted red/amber/green). Motion tokens too: `--ease-out`, `--ease-in-out`, `--ease-drawer`, `--t-*`. Name transition properties; never `transition: all`; hover motion behind `@media(hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)`; reduced-motion block at the end of the stylesheet.
- **Async views**: every view takes `const seq = renderStart();` and does `if(stale(seq)) return;` right after its await (the router replaces `#main` without notice). Tickers self-clear by checking their element still exists.
- **Form fields** have uppercase mini-labels (`.lbl`); submit buttons are guarded by `submitGuard()`; all lists use `fetchAll()` past PostgREST's 1000-row cap.
- **Hebrew** text goes through `bidi()` when next to Latin/dates.
- **Section shape (v50–v55, every section)**: a `.mast` masthead (title, one status line, one primary action, engraving at the side) → `.lead` tiles (the 2–3 numbers that matter today) → `.sec` blocks with `.ledger` rows (hairlines, no grey boxes, the whole `.lrow` is one tap target) → a detail sheet that holds Edit / Repeat / Delete. Forms open in sheets, never inline (generic helper: `openSheet({title, sub, body, foot, cls, focus})` / `closeSheet()`, one `#appBg` at a time; a `<button form="id">` in the foot submits the form in the body); no icon-button clusters on rows; destructive actions only inside a sheet behind `ask` (`delRow` returns true on success so the sheet knows to close). Review that led to this: `../Claude outputs/atlas-v49-review.md`.
- **Screens inside a section (v72)**: every section (House excepted) is a small app — `subnav(section, [[key,label],…])` draws a segmented control under the masthead and `pane(section, key, html)` wraps each screen; all screens are in the DOM so the render functions keep writing into their ids, only one has `.on`. `paneInit(section)` runs right after the section paints (sizes the sliding ink, restores the remembered screen); `paneShow(section, key)` switches, writes `#section/key` with `replaceState`, remembers in `localStorage['atlas_pane_<section>']`, and calls `charts[id].resize()` for canvases on the screen that opened (Chart.js draws nothing into a `display:none` canvas). `go()` and the hashchange handler accept `#gym/history`. The first screen is the section's *today*: lead tiles plus a short list ending in `moreRow(label, onclick)` that opens the full one; `foldRows(id, label, rows, open)` hides a run of rows (cashed rent months, older meter readings) behind one line. Screens: Schedule Today/Calendar/Upcoming · Gym Today/History/Progress/Records · Money Overview/Charges/Insights/Recurring (the Overview is always the running month; the month/all-time `scopeCtl()` sits on Charges and Insights) · Rent Checks/Electricity/Water · Goals Active/Completed · Chores Due/All · Wardrobe Figure/Closet/Matches (Closet's "All" caps each kind at four with an "All 23 t-shirts" line that sets `window._wdFilter='kind:<kind>'`). On phones the masthead `h2` is hidden (the top bar names the section). Checks: `verify72.js` in the build folder (213 checks at 390 and 1280).
- **Phone navigation (v65)**: under 760px the `.tabs` nav is a fixed bottom slider (z 40, under the live room at 50 and sheets at 60): each tab is a third of the width with `scroll-snap-align:center`, pseudo spacers let the first and last reach the middle, the open section is centred and full-size, its neighbours dimmed. `go()` calls `navCenter(smooth)` (instant when arriving from the hub); a swipe that settles on another tab calls `go()` from the scroll debounce / `scrollend`. `#topTitle` in the top bar names the open section. Desktop keeps the text strip. `#shell main` and `#toast` are padded above the bar; `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` is in the bar's padding. Tab markup is `<span class="ti">svg</span><span class="tl">Label</span>` — keep both when adding a section, and add the matching hub tile.

## Gym

- Live session draft in `localStorage['atlas_gym_draft']`; one batched Supabase write on Finish (`finishSession`). Items: `{ex, sets, reps, kg, done, keep}`; cardio stores minutes/km in reps/weight_kg with sets=1 and is excluded from volume/PRs.
- **Workout types** (v57): `workouts.kind` is 'A' (push), 'B' (pull) or 'legs', nullable; older rows are classified at runtime by `inferKind()` from their exercises' groups (Arms split by name: curls pull, the rest push). Start → `kindSheet()` lists the three types with their template and marks the one that has waited longest ("Up next"); `kindTemplate(kind)` = exercises shared by at least half of that type's last four sessions, in the latest session's order, prefilled from `lastFor()`; `KINDS[k].def` is the fallback list. The picker opens pre-ticked, so the template is always adjustable. Titles use `kindTitle(notes, kind)` ("Workout A", "Legs", or the notes).
- Flow: Start → type sheet → picker sheet → Start opens the **live room** (`liveOpen()`, full-screen `.live`, z 50; picker sheet is z 60, `ask`/finish sheets z 70). Set dots + "Set N done" auto-start the rest timer (`restStart`, wall-clock end stored in the draft). Minimise keeps it running; `views.gym()` reopens it unless `_liveMin`. Finish review (`finishSheet`) saves ticked sets, skips untouched exercises unless kept; if nothing was ticked, planned numbers save. `workouts.duration_min` is written (column exists).
- **Exercise pictograms** are inline SVG in `EX_SVG` (`[svg, restsOnEnd]`), generated — do not hand-edit them. Source: `Claude outputs/pictograms/` next to the repo (`gen.js` rig + `catalogue.js` poses; `node catalogue.js` → `all-svg.json`, then splice into `EX_SVG`). Grammar: 64 box, 3px round-cap strokes, filled r4.2 head, 5px pads/plates, 2.5px cables; barbell = level bar with end plates, dumbbell = bar + two plates; lying and hip-loaded lifts in profile (Bench Press, Skull Crusher, Hip Thrust) use `barE`, the plate seen end-on as a ring, so the bar never runs across the face or through the body. `catalogue.js` writes `all-svg.json` next to itself (or `$OUT`); splice with a script that rebuilds the whole `EX_SVG` block and keeps the `[svg, restsOnEnd]` pair (v63 restored the flag, which an earlier splice had dropped). Motion is the exercise's own range of motion, one `exMv` keyframe driven by `--r/--tx/--ty/--sx` on nested `.mv` groups; since v66 it runs wherever a figure is shown (Benji wanted every figure moving; only reduced-motion pauses it). Every entry must move, including `_fallback` (a lifting dumbbell) — Plank is a leg-raise hold, Shrug lifts the shoulders with a torso stub under the hips, Jump Rope hops with the rope narrowing (`slide` carries a hand-written `.mv` group). `EX_CAT` (name, group) and `EX_ALIAS` (typed names → catalogue) define the library; `exCanon()` folds names. `ex/*.png` are legacy and unused.

## Money

Apple Pay taps arrive via the `apple-pay` Edge Function (`supabase/functions/apple-pay/`, bearer token hashed in `webhook_tokens`); `merchantKey()` is duplicated in `index.html` and the function — change both. Categories are learned (`merchant_rules`), edits backfill sibling taps. PDF statement importer (pdf.js) dedupes on date+amount.

## Testing

`?fixture=demo|worst|empty|one|huge` loads `dev-fixtures.js`, an in-memory Supabase stand-in with a toggle bar — use it for any UI work instead of the live database. Headless-Chromium harnesses in past sessions stubbed the CDN scripts (`supabase-js`, `chart.js`, `pdf.js`) via `page.route`; Chart.js needs a `Chart.defaults` stub or the script dies before the router's `const`s initialise.

## Wardrobe (v67–v73)

Everything Benji owns, pulled once from his Terminal X (23 orders, Nov 2022 → Oct 2026), adidas and Nike order histories, in `wardrobe_items` (85 rows seeded server-side on 2026-11-01: 77 kept, 5 `returned`, 3 `gift`); saved looks in `outfits` (`slots` jsonb: head/outer/top/bottom/feet → item id). SQL in `supabase/wardrobe.sql`; `WARDROBE_SEED` in index.html is the same import (the empty state offers to insert it); `WARDROBE_PICKS` are seven in-stock matches found on 1 Nov 2026. Photos are hot-linked from the shops (Terminal X `…/catalog/product/cache/92af6b…/` = 360×480, `f112238e…` = 1200×1600, both generated on request so they work for discontinued products; adidas `assets.adidas.com`; Nike `static.nike.com`). Colours: the shop's Hebrew colour name through `WD_COLORS`; `WD_KIND` maps kind → slot.

**The figure is a 2D paper doll (v69)** — the three.js mannequin of v67/v68 is gone. `wdFigureSvg()` draws a front-view figure to Benji's numbers in a 130 × 200 cm stage (`WD_BODY`: 189 cm, shoulder line 155, waist 112, hip 98, crotch 86, knee 52; chest/waist/hip widths from his XL / waist-34 charts) with a cm ruler; head, neck and hands are a second SVG on top so hoods and collars sit behind them. Each worn piece is the shop's own flat product photo fetched through the `img` Edge Function (`supabase/functions/img/`, verify_jwt ON — the app sends the session token; only the four shop hosts), cut out of its white by an edge-connected flood (`wdCutout`, transparent PNGs handled too) with a thin ink outline so white pieces read on paper, then scaled so its cut-out height equals the piece's length in cm and placed by slot: tops hang from the shoulder line by their body length (hoodie: `body` 74 under a 100 cm total that includes the hood), bottoms from a 106 cm waistband, shoes on the ground as a mirrored side-view pair at ±13 cm, hats on the brow. Lengths: `length_cm`/`width_cm` on the row when Benji has taped the piece, else `WD_DIMS[kind]` ± `WD_SIZE_ADJ[size]` and the piece is tagged *estimated* (the shops publish body charts only, no garment lengths). "Flat lay" (`#wdFlat`) shows the worn pieces side by side with their cm. Tap on the doll hit-tests the cut-outs' alpha (`wdDollTap`). If the proxy fails the raw photo is shown with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` and no measurement. Cut-outs are cached per item in `W2.cache` (invalidate on edit). What the doll wears lives in `localStorage['atlas_wd_fit']`. `hero-wardrobe.png` is referenced but not drawn yet. Tests: `verify.js` (CDNs stubbed, the proxy answered with synthetic flat-lay photos from `testimg/`).

**Photos are prepared once, not cut on every visit (v70).** Terminal X numbers a product's gallery `-1…` to `-8…` in the file name (`WD_TX_RE`; a missing number answers a 720 × 900 "TERMINAL X" placeholder, not a 404) and the clean ghost shot is in there for nearly every piece but rarely first, on a 240–243 grey backdrop (not white) with a soft neutral drop shadow. **Prepare photos** (`wdPrepareAll`, the mast button shows while unprepared pieces remain) loads each piece's gallery through the proxy, drops placeholders, scores every shot (`wdScore`: backdrop colour and how much of the border it covers from `wdBgOf`, garment share, MediaPipe person/clothes share) and `wdPickShot` takes the first flat shot — nobody in it, framed on a light backdrop, garment 6–80 % of the frame (a fabric close-up fills it) — within a tenth of the best garment share (front before back); failing that the full-figure model shot with the most clothes; shoes and bags are never segmented. `wdCutout(bmp, kind, mode)` floods the backdrop from the edges at the sampled colour (tolerance 20 flat / 30 model) and, in flat mode, walks *down* neutral ramps (r = g = b ± 3, each step −3…+1) so the drop shadow goes but an off-white tee, which carries a cast and meets the backdrop in a step, stays; specks under 2 % of the largest piece are dropped. The PNG goes to Storage bucket `wardrobe` (private, owner-only policies, `<uid>/<item id>.png`) and the row keeps `cut_path, cut_w, cut_h, photo_kind` (flat / model / manual) and `photos` (the gallery urls); `wdPiece` then just downloads the PNG (IndexedDB-cached) — no segmentation at render time. The detail sheet shows the photo kind and offers Prepare/Redo photo and, for Terminal X pieces, Change photo (any gallery shot → `wdPrepareOne(x, url)`). Fixtures (`?fixture=demo`, no Storage) still cut at runtime. Validation: `mk-pick-test.js` in the build folder assembles the real functions into a snippet that runs in Chrome on a `media.terminalx.com` page (same-origin pixels) over four galleries; checked 2026-11-01: LFC tee, Mango trousers, Mango shirt → flat; Quiksilver (no ghost shot) → model.

**v71 — the prepare pass made robust after the first live run** (10 of 77 pieces done when Benji looked; three model-shot cuts had kept the whole frame). `wdPickShot` now returns every usable candidate in order (flats as before; then model shots with *some skin and clothes under 60 % of the frame* — the full figure, not a torso close-up — within a fifth of the most clothes in gallery order, then the rest), and `wdPrepareOne` cuts them in turn until one is sane: `wdCutout` reports `wdFill` (share of the frame kept, must be 2–85 %) and `wdParts` (big pieces ≥ 10 % of the largest, must be 1 unless shoes/bag — a model's arm across a tee splits it); the first whole one wins, else the first sane one. In model mode the garment step now runs whenever clothes were found (a cropped shot may show no skin). `wdPrepareAll` keeps going when he leaves the section (re-draws the doll only when it is on screen); the mast button reads Preparing… and is disabled meanwhile. Nike photos are fetched at `t_PDP_1280_v1` instead of `t_default`. Checked in Chrome: RVCA logo tee → image 4 (image 1 split in two by the arm), Billabong pocket tees → whole front shots.

**v73 — a piece without a clean photo is drawn, never shown as a ragged cut.** `wdDrawn(x, d)` returns an ink-and-paper garment SVG of the piece's kind (tee / polo / shirt / sweat / hoodie with hood above the shoulder line / jacket / pants / shorts / shoes side view toe-left / cap / beanie / box) in a viewBox of its cm, tinted with `x.color`, with faint seam lines; `wdDrawnEl` makes the node. `wdDollRender` and `wdFlatRender` use it whenever `wdPiece` has no canvas (`.wd-g.drawn`; the old raw-photo `multiply` fallback is gone), and the feet pair mirrors it like a cut-out. `wdPickShot` only cuts model shots on a light backdrop (`bright > 200` — `framed` is no test: studio gradient shots and a hoodie filling the frame score 0.3–0.5, the lifestyle shot that broke the Agave tee scored 121/0); when no candidate cuts, `wdPrepareOne` writes `photo_kind = 'drawn', cut_path = null` and `wdPrepareAll` skips such pieces unless forced; the detail sheet says "No clean shop photo · drawn to its measurements". Checks: `verify73.js` (verify72 + the doll wearing five drawn pieces in the fixture, 223 checks).

## Deals (v74)

What the daily gatherers found on sale, ranked for Benji. Backend: `supabase/deals.sql` (`deal_products`, `deal_prices` one row per product per day, `deal_runs`, `deal_flags`, view `deal_today` = one row per active product with today's price, `prev_price`, `lowest_price`, `days_seen`, his flag). Writes come only through the `deals-ingest` Edge Function (`supabase/functions/deals-ingest/`, verify_jwt off, bearer token hashed in `webhook_tokens` label `deals-runner`; POST `{shop, runner, run_id?, items[], seen, final|error}`, batches of ≤500, `final` retires what the shop no longer lists). The app writes only `deal_flags` (heart = `wish`, cross = `hidden`).

Gatherer: `scraper/` (Node 20, no Playwright — plain fetch + regex/JSON; `lib/common.js` holds Benji's sizes, the brand list, kinds, the tech wish-list and the score; one adapter per platform). `.github/workflows/deals.yml` runs six shops daily at 05:00 UTC (Redback/Outline/Benda = WooCommerce Store API, Terminal X = SSR brand pages + product JSON, Nike = wall `__NEXT_DATA__` + `next` link + `available_gtins`, Mous = Shopify products.json) with `DEALS_TOKEN` as the repo secret; `workflow_dispatch` takes `shops` and `dry`. Gant (WooCommerce behind Cloudflare), adidas (SFCC behind Akamai) and KSP (own JSON API behind Cloudflare) block datacenter IPs, so `node scraper/build-browser.js` bundles them into `scraper/browser/<shop>.js`, self-contained scripts a scheduled Claude task runs inside that shop's tab in Benji's Chrome (`window.__DEALS_TOKEN = "…"` then `(0, eval)(file)`; a caller-side `const` is invisible to the indirect eval; returns at once, poll `window.__DEALS_LAST`). Recon notes and KSP category ids: `scraper/RECON.md`. adidas's Product-Variation endpoint 403s after bursts — the adapter sleeps 1.2–2 s and backs off a minute on 403. KSP has no "was" price: its deals are sale labels or a price below our own history.

UI: hub tile + tab "Deals", screens Today (lead tiles, category/sort/shop chips, `My size` toggle, `.dl-card` grid, Show more), Watchlist (hearts with change since hearted, hidden list with Show again), History (`deal_prices` lines for products seen more than once). `dlDetail` opens the sheet with every gallery photo (`.dl-gal`, scroll-snap), sizes (his marked, sold-out struck), the shop's description/spec, the price line. `dlIsDeal` decides what shows on Today: a markdown, a sale flag, or a price below yesterday's. `hero-deals.png` is referenced but not drawn yet. Fixtures carry `deal_today`/`deal_prices`/`deal_runs`. Checks: `verify74.js` (46 checks at 1280 and 390).
